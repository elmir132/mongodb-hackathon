# Atlas launch — go-to-market memo

From: Marketing
To: Launch working group
Prepared: September 26, 2026
Status: Working draft

## Summary

The launch campaign will focus on how Atlas helps small teams turn scattered project updates into a shared source of truth. The landing page and customer email should lead with the practical benefit: less time chasing people for the latest answer.

## Rollout

We are targeting a public launch on Friday, October 2. The customer email will go out at 9:00 a.m. Eastern, followed by the social announcement and partner newsletter. We should make the signup page available just before the email lands so the links can be checked one last time.

The first audience is existing waitlist subscribers. Broader outreach can follow once we have reviewed the first wave of feedback. We will keep the launch-day messaging focused on the core workflow rather than every feature on the roadmap.

## Messaging and assets

The draft headline is “Keep your team on the same page, even when the plan changes.” The walkthrough should show a familiar project update, the context behind it, and a clear next step. Screenshots still need a final pass to make sure the product terminology matches the website.

Customer support will receive a short FAQ covering onboarding, project access, and how to report confusing results. Sales can reuse the same screenshots and customer-facing language in follow-up conversations.

## Before this goes out

Please review the memo for gaps, risky assumptions, and inconsistencies with previous project decisions. We still need an owner for watching the shared support inbox during the first few hours, and a clear fallback message if we have to pause the announcement.
